Curious by nature, efficient by habit.

I studied industrial design at Wentworth Institute of Technology, where I learned to care about how something gets made as much as how it gets used. Running client projects at Print Boston, from first request to final delivery, taught me to keep the details straight when plans change.

I also played varsity basketball at Wentworth. Moving from a team schedule into a 9-to-5 made me notice how much of my own behavior had depended on the structure around me. Why people do what they do, and what shapes their decisions, is what pulled me toward research.

Now I’m pursuing an M.S. in Human Factors in Information Design at Bentley University and work at Bentley’s User Experience Center, running usability testing, contextual ride-alongs, and workshops on interviewing and journey mapping. I’m most interested in where user experience meets business and emerging technology, and in turning complicated findings into recommendations a team can act on. Outside of work, I coach high school athletes.

How I work
