About Me

I’m naturally curious about people, how they think, what influences their decisions, and how they adapt to the systems around them.

My path to UX research wasn’t a straight line. I studied Industrial Design at Wentworth, where I learned that good design isn’t just about aesthetics. It’s about understanding how people interact with products and creating solutions that fit naturally into their lives. Around the same time, working at Print Boston taught me how to manage competing priorities, communicate with different stakeholders, and stay adaptable when plans inevitably changed.

Basketball shaped me just as much as design. As a varsity athlete, I relied on the structure of practices, games, and team accountability. When that structure disappeared after graduation, I became fascinated by how our environments influence behavior and decision-making. Coaching high school athletes only deepened that curiosity. Watching people build habits, respond to challenges, and grow in different environments made me want to better understand the factors that shape our experiences.

Today, I’m pursuing an M.S. in Human Factors in Information Design at Bentley University and working as a Design & Research Associate at Bentley’s User Experience Center. Through research projects, field studies, and collaborative workshops, I’m learning how to turn observations into meaningful insights that help teams make better decisions.

I’m particularly interested in the intersection of human behavior, business, and emerging technology. As an active AI adopter, I regularly experiment with coding agents and AI tools to prototype ideas, accelerate learning, and create more effective ways of working. While technology continues to evolve, I’m most interested in how it can support people and make complex experiences easier to navigate.

How I Work

Curious

I pay attention to the moments when expectations and reality don’t align. Those moments often reveal the most meaningful opportunities for improvement.

Collaborative

The best insights rarely come from one perspective. I enjoy working closely with teammates, comparing observations, and building understanding together.

Analytical

I look for patterns before conclusions, separating what I observed from what I think it means.

Adaptable

Whether plans change, timelines shift, or new information emerges, I’m comfortable adjusting course and finding a path forward.

Detail-Oriented

Small decisions shape experiences. I enjoy digging into the nuances that help products feel more intuitive and cohesive.

Systems Thinker

I look beyond individual interactions to understand how people, processes, tools, and environments influence the broader experience.
